DejaVuSans.ttf is an international TrueType font from the DejaVu font package
(DejaVu Sans version 2.32, DejaVu fonts team). It is distributed under the
conditions of the Bitstream Vera Fonts Copyright and, for the glyphs imported
from the Arev fonts, of the Arev Fonts Copyright. The full text of both
licences is embedded in the font file itself, in the licence entry of its name
table.

The DejaVu fonts are a font family based on the Bitstream Vera Fonts
(http://gnome.org/fonts/). Its purpose is to provide a wider range of
characters while maintaining the original look and feel.
