uimwTarget

Name + Title: Name of your target as well as their title (or Function in the case of a non-player character).

Life: Life (hit points) of your target.

Level: The round badge on the right shows the level of the target. The level and the ring around it take the color of the level band of the target (see below). Above the level, a figure followed by a star, from 1x to 5x, tells how strong the target is within its band. One black star marks a named creature or a mini-boss, a challenge for a team, and the ring turns gray-green or brown depending on its rank; two black stars mark a boss, a challenge for a guild, and the ring turns yellow, or white for a boss that is a non-player character.

When the target is more than 10 levels above your level, plus 10 levels for each of your team mates, its level stays hidden and the strength sign takes its place, in red for an ordinary creature. No level is shown for players, nor for targets you cannot attack.

Level bands by color:
- 1-19 Green (Beginner areas)
- 20-49 Blue
- 50-99 Yellow
- 100-149 Orange
- 150-199 Red
- 200+ Purple
